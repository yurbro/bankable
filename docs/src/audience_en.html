Reader-aware: one model, different chapter weights and metrics

Pick the primary reader first: it decides the first sentence of the executive summary and which chapters expand. Other readers get a tailored summary plus dedicated chapters. Never two sets of numbers.

Equity investors

First questionWhy is this a good business, why you, why now?
  • Bottom-up TAM / SAM / SOM
  • Moat and competitive matrix
  • Unit economics and traction
  • Use of funds, milestones, valuation logic
MetricsRevenue CAGR · gross margin · EBITDA break-even year · CAC/LTV · runway · IRR (with terminal value)

Banks / lenders

First questionHow is the loan repaid — and what if it isn't?
  • Repayment source, DSCR by year
  • Borrower standing, historical financials
  • Use of proceeds, collateral, mitigants
  • Conservative case and 12-month cash flow
MetricsDSCR ≥ 1.2–1.5 · interest coverage · debt-to-asset · minimum cash · downside repayment capacity

Government grants / parks

First questionWhy support it — which policy does it serve, what benefits does it bring?
  • Policy basis (document and clause)
  • Scope, total investment, funding incl. matching funds
  • Quantified economic / social / environmental benefits
  • Performance targets and schedule
MetricsTotal investment · investment intensity · output and tax at full capacity · jobs · energy and emissions · payback · matching ratio

Boards / partners

First questionShould we do it, with what resources, what if we don't, what if it fails?
  • One-sentence decision request
  • Alternatives compared (incl. not proceeding)
  • Resources, budget, decision gates
  • Risks and stop-loss conditions
MetricsNPV · IRR · payback · peak funding · ±20% sensitivity · impact on group cash
Several readersOne master document for the primary reader → a one-page tailored summary per secondary reader → add "debt service and collateral" for banks, "policy basis + benefits and performance table" for government, "alternatives and decision request" for boards.
